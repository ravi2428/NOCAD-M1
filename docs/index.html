NOCAD — M-1 Reader

Embedded multi-spectral point-of-care optical reader and offline validation engine for rapid food safety screening. Architecture follows the SIH6235 technical report v4.2: reusable controlled reader + matrix-specific disposable cartridge.

Lane A
Competitive AuNP AFM1 lateral-flow measurement.
Lane B
Colourimetric adulterant spot array + blank/reference.
Reader
Pi Zero 2 W, controlled optics, thermal interface and local processing.
Decision order
Quality gate first, quantitation second.

Measurement pipeline

4-corner fiducials→Homography→Lighting correction→CIELAB / ΔE→Matrix validity→4PL→Decision→Signed ledger
Important: the current “offline AI” block is not a trained AI model. It is deterministic measurement plus a Mahalanobis statistical distance check. Calibration coefficients and validity limits are placeholders until real-cartridge laboratory validation.

Hardware intent

Optics
Sealed dark chamber, fixed geometry, high-CRI regulated LED source, diffuser and matte baffles.
Thermal
Kapton heater + aluminium spreader + SHT31-D feedback. Current engineering target: 40.0 °C; final assay set point is validation-dependent.
Edge compute
Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W. The reported ~0.7 s processing figure is a laptop measurement, not a Pi benchmark.
Power
USB-C regulated supply with protection. Battery life is a design target pending the final heater duty cycle.

Verdict semantics

PASS — screened RETEST — grey zone SUSPECT — follow-up INVALID — do not interpret

A SUSPECT screening result is not legal evidence by itself. The original food must enter the applicable formal sampling and confirmatory laboratory workflow.
