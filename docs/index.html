04 / Reusable reader

Controlled measurement hardware

The physical reader is designed to remove uncontrolled lighting, geometry and temperature as major sources of measurement variation.

Optical chamber

Sealed dark bay, fixed camera geometry, matte baffles and regulated high-CRI illumination. Optional diffuser/micro-diffuser controls spatial uniformity.

Thermal control

Kapton heater, aluminium spreader and SHT31-D feedback. 40.0 °C is an engineering target in the current design, not a validated assay optimum.

Edge compute

Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W performs local control and analysis. No cloud service is required for the core measurement decision.

Traceability

Cartridge identity, calibration context and result record are designed to be retained locally with a tamper-evident hash/signature chain.

05 / Decision layer

Four possible measurement states

The verdict describes the screening state. It does not replace statutory sampling or accredited laboratory confirmation.

PASS · Screened

Measurement passes the defined quality and screening criteria. It is a screening result, not a universal legal declaration.

RETEST · Grey zone

Signal is near a decision boundary or needs another controlled measurement before interpretation.

SUSPECT · Follow-up

Potential contamination/adulteration requiring formal sampling and confirmatory laboratory analysis.

INVALID · Do not interpret

Quality, temperature, geometry, matrix or other validity checks indicate the measurement should not be used.

06 / Scientific guardrails

What this architecture does — and does not claim

Screening platform

Designed for rapid field screening. Suspect samples proceed to the applicable formal sampling and confirmatory laboratory pathway.

Not “AI from a photo”

The current implementation is controlled image measurement plus statistical validity checking. Do not present it as a trained diagnostic AI model.

Performance is to validate

LOD, sensitivity, specificity, false-negative rate, recovery, precision, stability and field performance require experimental validation.

Integration is the novelty

The architecture integrates trace AFM1 screening, chemical adulterant screening, controlled optics, validity gating and signed traceability around one field workflow.
